Killing Floor

Author: Lee Child

Originally Published: March 1997

Genre: Novel, Fiction, Mystery, Thriller

Publisher: Bantam Press (UK), G. P. Putnam's Sons (US)

Summary: Killing Floor is the debut novel by Lee Child, first published in 1997. It introduces the character Jack Reacher, a former military policeman who gets caught up in a murder investigation in a small Georgia town.
